About Rancho Santa Margarita

Rancho Santa Margarita sits in the Saddleback Valley foothills of south Orange County, bordered by Mission Viejo, Lake Forest, Laguna Niguel, and San Juan Capistrano, with Coto de Caza and Trabuco Canyon just east.

Incorporated in 2000 and master-planned under the SAMLARC association, much like neighboring Aliso Viejo, most of its homes date to the 1990s and 2000s. Residents gather at Lago Santa Margarita and the SAMLARC Beach Club, the RSM Town Center, and Central Park, with the Tijeras Creek and Dove Canyon golf clubs, O'Neill Regional Park, and the Saddleback Mountains framing the city.

Warm inland summers push refrigerators, freezers, and garage units hard, and because so many kitchens still run their original built-in appliances, now 15 to 25 years old, we handle a steady mix of Sub-Zero and Thermador work, plus Bosch dishwashers, alongside everyday Whirlpool, Samsung, and LG repairs.

Common Appliance Problems We Fix

Most repairs are finished in a single visit. These are the symptoms we diagnose every day across Rancho Santa Margarita.

  • Refrigerator not cooling or struggling in summer heat
  • Garage refrigerator or freezer running warm in hot weather
  • Washer not draining or leaving clothes soaked
  • Dryer taking two cycles to finish a load
  • Dishwasher not draining or leaving standing water
  • Oven not heating or baking unevenly
  • Cooktop burner not igniting or sparking
  • Refrigerator ice maker not producing ice
  • Freezer warm while the fridge section stays cold
  • Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
  • Wine cooler not holding its set temperature
  • Microwave running but not heating
