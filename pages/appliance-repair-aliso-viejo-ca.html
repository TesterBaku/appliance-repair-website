About Aliso Viejo

Aliso Viejo sits in the Saddleback Valley foothills of south Orange County, bordered by Laguna Niguel, Laguna Beach, Lake Forest, and Mission Viejo. Incorporated in 2001, it is one of the county's youngest cities and was master-planned from the ground up, much like neighboring Rancho Santa Margarita, so most homes date to the 1990s and 2000s. Residents gather at the Aliso Viejo Town Center, Grand Park and the Aliso Viejo Aquatic Center, and the trails of Aliso and Wood Canyons Wilderness Park, with Soka University and the Aliso Viejo Country Club nearby. Because so many kitchens and laundry rooms still run their original built-in appliances, now 15 to 25 years old, we see a steady mix of Sub-Zero and Thermador work, plus Bosch dishwashers, alongside everyday Whirlpool, Samsung, and LG repairs across the city.

Common Appliance Problems We Fix

Most repairs are finished in a single visit. These are the symptoms we diagnose every day across Aliso Viejo.

  • Refrigerator not cooling or running too warm
  • Built-in Sub-Zero or Bosch fridge failing after 15+ years
  • Washer not draining or leaving clothes soaked
  • Dryer taking two cycles to finish a load
  • Dishwasher not draining or leaving standing water
  • Oven not heating or baking unevenly
  • Cooktop burner not igniting or sparking
  • Refrigerator ice maker not producing ice
  • Freezer warm while the fridge section stays cold
  • Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
  • Wine cooler not holding its set temperature
  • Microwave running but not heating
