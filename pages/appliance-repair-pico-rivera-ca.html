About Pico Rivera

Pico Rivera was formed when the neighboring communities of Pico and Rivera merged, and about 62,000 people lived here at the 2020 census. The Pico Rivera Sports Arena has hosted Mexican rodeos and concerts since 1979, and the Pico Rivera Historical Museum occupies a former railroad depot dating from the late 1880s. Our recent work here runs from a Samsung dryer and a refrigerator coil cleaning to Viking and Dacor ranges and a Viking dishwasher, all shown on this page. Pico Rivera is also served by two water providers, the Pico Rivera Water Authority and Pico Water District, and Pico Water District's water is noticeably harder; our LA County hard water guide compares them and explains what it means for your dishwasher.

Common Appliance Problems We Fix

Most repairs are finished in a single visit. These are the symptoms we diagnose every day in Pico Rivera homes.

  • Refrigerator not cooling or running warm
  • Freezer warm while the fridge section stays cold
  • Washer not draining or leaving clothes soaked
  • Dryer taking two cycles to finish a load
  • Dishwasher not draining or leaving standing water
  • Viking or Dacor range not powering on
  • Oven not heating or holding temperature
  • Gas cooktop burner not lighting or sparking
  • Oven convection fan noisy or not running
  • Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
  • Ice maker not producing or dropping ice
  • Wine cooler not holding its set temperature
