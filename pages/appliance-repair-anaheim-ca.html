Tell Us What Your Appliance Is Doing

Refrigerator or freezer running warm

Tell us whether the refrigerator, freezer or both sections have stopped cooling, and whether the ice maker is affected. Mention whether the appliance is freestanding or built into cabinetry. Your appliance needs its own diagnosis before we recommend parts.

Washer not draining or dryer not heating

Describe where the cycle stops and any message on the display. For a dryer, tell us whether it starts, tumbles and produces heat. Mention stacked or closet installations so we can discuss access.

Dishwasher, oven or stove problems

Standing water, dishes left dirty, an oven that will not heat or a burner that will not light are reasons to request a diagnosis. Have the model number and error code ready if available. You do not need to remove appliance panels for us.
