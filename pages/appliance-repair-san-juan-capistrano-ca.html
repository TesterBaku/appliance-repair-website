About San Juan Capistrano

San Juan Capistrano sits along San Juan Creek in south Orange County, bordered by Dana Point, San Clemente, and Rancho Santa Margarita. Founded in 1776 with the building of Mission San Juan Capistrano, the seventh of California's 21 missions and famous for the swallows' annual March 19 return, the city is often called the birthplace of Orange County, even though it was not incorporated until 1961. That span, from the adobes of the Los Rios Historic District, California's oldest residential street, to the 1894 Mission Revival Amtrak and Metrolink depot across the tracks, to the newer hillside tracts of San Juan Hills and Pacifica San Juan, means we see everything from century-old original kitchens to modern built-in appliances. Because so many homes near the Mission and Los Rios sit alongside newer premium builds, our technicians regularly work on Sub-Zero, Wolf, and Thermador units here, right alongside the Whirlpool, Samsung, and LG appliances that fill most other kitchens in town.
