What Affects the Oven & Stove Repair Cost?

Six variables explain most of the price variation between otherwise similar oven and stove repairs.

  1. The specific failure. A worn igniter or temperature sensor costs far less than a control board replacement. The root cause matters more than the symptom; a proper diagnosis pins this down before any commitment is made.
  2. Freestanding range vs. wall oven + cooktop. A wall oven paired with a separate cooktop typically means more disassembly to reach shared wiring and controls than a single freestanding range. Freestanding units are generally easier to access, keeping labor costs lower on many repairs.
  3. Brand and parts availability. Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, and LG parts are widely stocked, enabling same-day repairs at predictable cost. Wolf, Viking, Thermador, and Dacor parts are often proprietary and may need to be ordered, adding time and parts cost.
  4. Age of the appliance. Older units may have multiple worn components. Secondary issues sometimes surface after the primary repair; we flag these in the estimate before any work starts so you can decide how to proceed.
  5. Repair complexity. Replacing a temperature sensor is a 30-minute job. Replacing a control board on a convection oven involves more disassembly and recalibration. Labor time scales with how deep the technician has to go.
  6. Parts on the truck. Common parts for the most popular brands ride on our service vehicles, enabling same-visit repairs at no added cost. Specialty or luxury-brand parts may require a follow-up appointment after ordering.

Repair or Replace? A Practical Guide for Ovens & Stoves

The 50% rule: if the repair cost exceeds 50% of a comparable new oven/range installed, replacement is usually the smarter call. Ovens and ranges typically last about 14.5 years for gas models and 16.8 years for electric, per Department of Energy service-life data.

Lean toward repair when…

Repair Makes Sense

  • Oven or range is under 10 years old
  • Repair cost is under 50% of replacement
  • The failure is a single part (element, igniter, sensor, hinge)
  • The brand is premium (Wolf, Viking, Thermador), where replacement cost is high
  • You've been happy with its cooking and baking performance
Lean toward replacement when…

Replacement Makes Sense

  • Oven or range is over 12 years old
  • Repair cost exceeds 50% of a new unit installed
  • This is the second major repair in two years
  • The control board or main burner assembly has failed on an older unit
  • Parts are discontinued or no longer available
