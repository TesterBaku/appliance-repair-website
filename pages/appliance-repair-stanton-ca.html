About Stanton

Stanton sits in the middle of northern Orange County, bordered by Cypress to the west, Anaheim to the north and east, and Garden Grove to the east and south. At roughly three square miles it is one of the county's smallest cities, and Beach Boulevard, State Route 39, runs straight down the middle of it. Our own shop is here, at 10832 Asbury Avenue. Residents know Stanton Central Park on Western Avenue, Harry M. Dotson Park and its two-story pirate ship on Fern, the Norm Ross Sports Complex on Cedar, Hollenbeck Park on Cerritos, and Rodeo 39 Public Market on Beach. The housing is mostly older single-family homes, apartment buildings, and mobile home communities, which is exactly the mix that keeps refrigerators, washers, dryers, ovens, and dishwashers in service well past the point where a newer neighborhood would have replaced them.

Compact, Stacked, and Older Appliances

A lot of Stanton kitchens and laundry closets were never built for full-size appliances. Smaller houses, older apartment buildings, and the city's mobile home communities all tend toward narrower, shorter, and stacked units. Those are not just small versions of the same machine, and the difference matters on a service call.

Apartment-size and 20-inch ranges.
Smaller cavities use different bake elements, igniters, and thermostats than a 30-inch range, and the parts are not interchangeable.
Narrow-depth and top-freezer refrigerators.
Tighter cabinets mean less clearance for the condenser coils, so they run hotter and the defrost system works harder.
Stacked washer and dryer towers.
The whole unit usually has to come out of the closet before anything can be reached, which is a real factor in how long the visit takes.
Older gas ranges.
Igniters weaken gradually rather than failing outright, so the burner takes longer and longer to light before anyone calls it broken.
Under-counter and portable dishwashers.
Common in rentals and smaller kitchens, and they fail differently from a standard built-in.

Different parts, different clearances, and often a different diagnosis. We work on both, so it helps to say on the phone which one you have. The technician then arrives with the right parts on the van instead of ordering them afterward.

Common Appliance Problems We Fix

Most repairs are finished in a single visit. These are the symptoms we diagnose every day across Stanton.

  • Refrigerator not cooling or running too warm
  • Stacked washer and dryer tower not tumbling or draining
  • Washer not draining or leaving clothes soaked
  • Dryer taking two cycles to finish a load
  • Dishwasher not draining or leaving standing water
  • Oven not heating or baking unevenly
  • Older gas range with a weak or clicking igniter
  • Refrigerator ice maker not producing ice
  • Freezer warm while the fridge section stays cold
  • Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
  • Wine cooler not holding its set temperature
  • Microwave running but not heating
