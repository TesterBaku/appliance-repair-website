About La Habra

La Habra is an established, family-oriented city in the northeast corner of Orange County, bordered by Brea, Fullerton, and Buena Park, with Placentia and Yorba Linda a short drive east. Across the county line, we also serve neighboring Whittier, in Los Angeles County. It is anchored by the Children's Museum at La Habra in the historic Union Pacific depot, the La Habra Marketplace, Westridge Golf Club, and parks like Portola Park and La Bonita Park, with the Imperial Highway, Harbor Boulevard, and Whittier Boulevard corridors running through town. Because the housing spans several decades, the appliances age in waves: original built-ins and decades-old refrigerators, washers, and ranges make up most of what we repair here, alongside the premium built-ins in the newer Westridge and Country Hills kitchens.

Common Appliance Problems We Fix

Most repairs are completed in a single visit. Here are the symptoms we diagnose every day across La Habra.

  • Refrigerator not cooling or freezing unevenly
  • Samsung or LG ice maker not making ice
  • Washer not draining or leaving clothes soaking wet
  • Washer leaking from a worn door boot seal
  • Dryer taking two or more cycles to dry a load
  • Dishwasher not cleaning or leaving standing water
  • GE or Whirlpool oven not heating to the set temperature
  • Gas stove burner clicking but not lighting
  • Freezer running warm while the fridge stays cold
  • Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
  • Microwave not heating or turntable not turning
  • Original 1960s built-in appliance finally failing
