Appliance Problems We Fix

The three repairs above were a refrigerator that stopped cooling, an oven with no heat and a dryer that was not heating. Each of those symptoms can come from more than one part, which is why the first visit is a diagnostic. Here are the problems we repair.

Refrigerator warm, or one side not cooling
Freezer not freezing or frosting over
Ice maker not making ice, or water dispenser not working
Washer not draining, not spinning or leaking
Dryer running but not heating
Dishwasher not draining or not cleaning
Oven not heating or not holding temperature
Gas burner not lighting or clicking nonstop
Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
Wine cooler not holding its set temperature
