About Buena Park

Buena Park sits in northern Orange County, bordered by Fullerton, Anaheim, Garden Grove, and Cypress to the south, with the Los Angeles County line just to the north. It is best known for the Beach Boulevard entertainment corridor, home to Knott's Berry Farm, Knott's Soak City, Medieval Times Dinner & Tournament, and Pirates Dinner Adventure. Families shop and dine at Buena Park Downtown and spend weekends at Ralph B. Clark Regional Park. The city's housing runs from post-war tract neighborhoods with long-tenured family homes to newer infill and rental housing near the corridor, so we repair everything from decades-old refrigerators and ranges to late-model washers and dishwashers here.

Common Appliance Problems We Fix

Most repairs are completed in a single visit. Here are the symptoms we diagnose every day across Buena Park.

  • Refrigerator not cooling or freezing unevenly
  • Samsung or LG ice maker not making ice
  • Washer not draining or leaving clothes wet
  • Washer leaking from a worn door boot seal
  • Dryer taking two or more cycles to dry clothes
  • Dishwasher not cleaning or leaving standing water
  • GE or Whirlpool oven not heating to set temperature
  • Gas stove burner clicking but not lighting
  • Freezer running warm while fridge section is cold
  • Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
  • Microwave not heating or turntable not rotating
  • Older post-war built-in appliance finally failing
