About Montebello

Montebello sits in southeast Los Angeles County, a few miles east of downtown Los Angeles, with about 62,600 residents at the 2020 Census. The city's oldest structure, the Sanchez Adobe, was built in 1845 and still stands as a museum inside Sanchez Adobe Park, preserved together with the Montebello Historical Society. Families gather at Grant Rea Park, home to the Montebello Barnyard Zoo, and shop at the Shops at Montebello, the city's longtime regional mall. The city's original 1928 golf course, designed by Max Behr and purchased by the city in 1941, was completely rebuilt and reopened in 2024 as the city-owned Bella Verde Golf Club, now paired with a Topgolf. On the north edge of town, the undeveloped Montebello Hills still hold a long-running oil field, industrial history rather than a neighborhood we currently serve. That mix of century-old adobe homes, postwar tract housing along Whittier Boulevard, and newer builds near Via Campo and Beverly Boulevard is exactly the range of Whirlpool, LG, and premium built-in work we handle here, and in the nearby city of Whittier too.

Common Appliance Problems We Fix

A single visit typically resolves the problem. Here is what shows up most often in Montebello homes and kitchens.

  • Refrigerator running warm or not cooling
  • Freezer not holding temperature while the fridge stays cold
  • Washer stopping mid-cycle or not draining
  • Dryer running two cycles to finish one load
  • Dishwasher leaving standing water after a cycle
  • Oven not reaching or holding temperature
  • Gas cooktop burner clicking but not lighting
  • Range or oven convection fan running noisy
  • Garbage disposal humming without spinning
  • Ice maker not making or dropping ice
  • Wine cooler drifting off its set temperature
  • Built-in refrigerator door seal failing to close
