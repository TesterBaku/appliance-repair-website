Common Appliance Problems We Fix

Tell us what stopped working, the brand and any error code you see. These are the household appliance problems you can call us about.

  • Refrigerator not cooling or freezing unevenly
  • Ice maker not making ice or leaking inside the freezer
  • Wine cooler not reaching set temperature
  • Washer will not drain or spin, or leaves clothes wet
  • Dryer taking two or more cycles to dry clothes
  • Dishwasher not cleaning or leaving standing water
  • Oven not heating or Wolf range burner not lighting
  • Thermador cooktop with no spark or uneven heat
  • Freezer running warm while fridge section is cold
  • Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
  • Microwave not heating or turntable not rotating
  • Viking or Miele oven display error codes
