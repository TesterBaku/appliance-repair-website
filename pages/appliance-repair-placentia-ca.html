About Placentia

Placentia is an established, family-oriented city in north Orange County, bordered by Fullerton, Yorba Linda, and Brea. It blends the historic homes of Old Town Placentia with 1970s and 1980s suburban tracts, anchored by Tri-City Park, the Alta Vista Country Club, the historic Bradford House, and the Placentia Champions Sports Complex, with Cal State Fullerton just to the west and quick access to the SR-57 Orange Freeway. Because the housing spans several eras, the appliances age in waves: original built-ins and decades-old refrigerators, washers, and ranges are the bulk of what we repair here, alongside the premium built-ins in Alta Vista's newer kitchens.

Common Appliance Problems We Fix

Most repairs are completed in a single visit. Here are the symptoms we diagnose every day across Placentia.

  • Refrigerator not cooling or freezing unevenly
  • Samsung or LG ice maker not making ice
  • Washer not draining or leaving clothes wet
  • Washer leaking from a worn door boot seal
  • Dryer taking two or more cycles to dry clothes
  • Dishwasher not cleaning or leaving standing water
  • GE or Whirlpool oven not heating to set temperature
  • Gas stove burner clicking but not lighting
  • Freezer running warm while fridge section is cold
  • Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
  • Microwave not heating or turntable not rotating
  • Original 1970s built-in appliance finally failing
