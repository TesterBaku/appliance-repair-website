About Seal Beach

Seal Beach is a small, walkable coastal city in the northwest corner of Orange County, bordered by Huntington Beach, Westminster, and Garden Grove, with the Rossmoor community just to the north and Long Beach across the San Gabriel River. It is anchored by the historic Seal Beach Pier, one of the longest wooden piers in California, the shops along Main Street, Eisenhower Park, the Seal Beach National Wildlife Refuge, and the Old Ranch Country Club, with Pacific Coast Highway running along the coast. Because the housing ranges from Old Town beach cottages to the large Leisure World retirement community, the appliances we repair here run the full spectrum: compact, apartment-sized units in Leisure World, salt-exposed washers and refrigerators near the water, and premium built-ins in the College Park and waterfront homes.

Common Appliance Problems We Fix

Most repairs are completed in a single visit. Here are the symptoms we diagnose every day across Seal Beach.

  • Refrigerator not cooling or freezing unevenly
  • Samsung or LG ice maker not making ice
  • Washer not draining or leaving clothes soaking wet
  • Salt-air corrosion seizing a washer or dryer component
  • Dryer taking two or more cycles to dry a load
  • Dishwasher not cleaning or leaving standing water
  • GE or Whirlpool oven not heating to the set temperature
  • Gas stove burner clicking but not lighting
  • Freezer running warm while the fridge stays cold
  • Garbage disposal humming but not spinning
  • Compact or stacked laundry unit in a condo not draining
  • Older Leisure World built-in appliance finally failing
