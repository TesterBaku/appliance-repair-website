# PR #786 review artifacts

## Scope

PR #786 adds the Fullerton Empava cooktop replacement and Anaheim Monogram
refrigerator job evidence. It introduces twelve optimized image assets and
places them on related city, appliance, brand, and recent-repairs surfaces.

## Independent design assessments

Two independent read-only assessments reviewed commit `72aba57` before the PR.
They identified no P0 issue. One assessment found these actionable issues:

- two new GE service links were below the 44px mobile tap-target requirement;
- the two portrait job cards used only a 480px source despite a 768px source;
- the gallery's newly extended filter surface needed 44px chip controls.

Commit `8ceeea6` addressed those points by using 44px inline-flex CTAs,
44px filter chips, responsive 480w/768w portrait sources, and modest top
spacing on the Anaheim proof section.

Two fresh independent read-only reassessments then reviewed `8ceeea6`. Neither
found a P0 or P1 issue. One later concern that the GE hub lacked the Anaheim
card was independently checked against the committed HTML: the Anaheim Monogram
card is present in that hub alongside the Orange and Irvine cards, so no change
was required.
